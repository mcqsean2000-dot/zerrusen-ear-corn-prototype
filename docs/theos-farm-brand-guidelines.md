# Theo's Farm Brand Guidelines

This is the canonical brand reference for Theo's Farm website, packaging, Facebook, Instagram, and future social post creation.

## Brand position

Theo's Farm brings cleaned whole ear corn from a family farm directly to wildlife feeders. The brand should feel honest, established, practical, and unmistakably agricultural.

Brand promise: Real whole ears. Real farm handling. Straightforward choices in 20 lb and 40 lb bags.

## Official naming

- Public brand: **Theo's Farm**
- Website: **theosfarm.com**
- Product descriptor: **Whole Ear Corn**
- Supporting phrase: **Whole & Natural**
- Campaign phrase: **Farm to Feeder**

Always include the apostrophe and space in `Theo's Farm`. Use `theosfarm.com` only for the web address.

## Logo system

- Primary heritage logo: [`../assets/brand/theos-farm-primary-logo-concept.png`](../assets/brand/theos-farm-primary-logo-concept.png)
- Compact social badge: [`../assets/brand/theos-farm-badge-logo.png`](../assets/brand/theos-farm-badge-logo.png)
- Visual guide: [`../output/pdf/theos-farm-brand-guidelines.pdf`](../output/pdf/theos-farm-brand-guidelines.pdf)

Use the primary logo for storytelling, packaging concepts, website hero treatments, and larger social graphics. Use the compact badge for profile images, avatars, stickers, and small placements.

Keep clear space around either logo equal to the height of one `EAR CORN` capital letter. Minimum recommended sizes:

- Primary logo: 180 px digital or 1.75 inches in print.
- Badge: 64 px digital or 0.65 inches in print.

Do not retype or redraw the Theo's Farm script wordmark. Use the supplied artwork.

## Color palette

| Color | Hex | Primary use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field Forest | `#30451F` | Wordmark, leaves, line art |
| Harvest Gold | `#CF9418` | Corn, rules, highlights |
| Soil Brown | `#4A2E18` | `EAR CORN`, supporting accents |
| Warm Parchment | `#F1DFC0` | Primary heritage background |
| Cream | `#FBF5E9` | Clean layouts and open space |
| Charcoal | `#2B251E` | Body copy |

Use forest, soil, or charcoal text on parchment and cream. Harvest gold is for large type, icons, rules, and highlights, not small body copy.

## Typography

- Logo script: supplied artwork only.
- Headlines: Roboto Slab Condensed Bold or a similar sturdy condensed slab serif.
- Body copy: Inter Regular or Semibold.

Supporting typography should be clean and restrained so the heritage logo remains the focal point.

## Photography

When a visual represents the purchasable product, always show:

- Actual white 20 lb and 40 lb bags.
- Actual whole ears of corn on the cob.
- Real shipping boxes and real farm handling when relevant.

Never:

- Present a fictional brown retail bag as product photography.
- Show loose kernels as the product being sold.
- Alter ear size, fill, or color to imply unsupported quality claims.
- Let generated imagery override the appearance of the real product.

The logo may be nostalgic. Product photography must be literal.

## Social voice

Write in a natural farm-to-feeder voice:

- Say `whole ears of corn on the cob`, not loose kernels.
- Use specific, supportable details.
- Mention 20 lb and 40 lb options when useful.
- Sound like a working family farm, not a national feed conglomerate.
- Include a clear invitation to visit `https://theosfarm.com`.
- Avoid hype, unsupported superlatives, and excessive hashtags.

## Social visual pattern

- Place the primary logo or a short hook on the left or top.
- Place an unaltered real product photo on the right or bottom.
- Use the compact badge for profile images and small corner marks.
- Keep posts visually warm, agricultural, uncluttered, and legible.
- Clearly distinguish vintage brand artwork from actual product photography.

## Source prompts

The primary logo was created with the supplied fictional bag artwork as style inspiration only. The production prompt requested:

> A polished, original heritage-farm logo with an expressive dark forest-green script wordmark, one golden whole ear of field corn, crop rows, a Midwestern farmhouse and barn, bold condensed `EAR CORN` lettering, and `WHOLE & NATURAL`, using forest green, harvest gold, soil brown, and warm parchment.

The compact badge prompt simplified that system to the script wordmark, one whole ear with leaves, `EAR CORN`, and a circular border for small-format legibility.
